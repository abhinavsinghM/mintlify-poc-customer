<Note>
  This page explains how Magnus is built to handle your data at a high level. It isn't your organization's official privacy policy or a legal document — for the authoritative answer, check with your organization's IT/security team or your company's published privacy policy.
</Note>

## Your data stays within your organization

Magnus is built so that what you tell it, what it remembers about you, and what it searches on your behalf are scoped to you and your organization — not pooled with or visible to other companies using Magnus, and not shared between your organization's employees' individual conversations.

## What gets remembered, and where

- **Within a conversation**, everything you've said is available to Magnus for the rest of that conversation.
- **Across conversations**, only a smaller set of durable facts is carried forward — see [What Magnus Remembers](/collaboration/memory) for the distinction, and how to ask Magnus to forget something.

## Files you share

Files you attach are used to help with your request. If Magnus produces a file for you, it's delivered back to you directly in the conversation rather than posted somewhere public.

## Connected tools use your own access

When Magnus acts in a tool on your behalf (see [Connected Tools](/capabilities/connected-tools)), it does so using your own connection to that tool where applicable — it doesn't give you access to anything you didn't already have, and actions taken show up as coming from you.

## Questions about compliance or specific policies

For anything specific — data retention periods, regulatory compliance, how to request deletion of your data — reach out to your organization's IT, security, or people team. They'll have the authoritative answer for your company's setup.
