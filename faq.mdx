---
title: "FAQ"
description: "Quick answers to common questions about using Magnus"
---

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Do I need to know which 'agent' or system to ask?">
    No. Ask Magnus directly, in plain language — it works out what kind of question or task you have and handles the routing itself. You never need to know or choose between the specialists behind it.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Will Magnus remember what I told it last week?">
    Sometimes — durable, useful facts about you are carried forward across conversations, but not everything you've ever said. See [What Magnus Remembers](/collaboration/memory) for the distinction.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can Magnus see other people's conversations or data?">
    No. What Magnus knows about you and remembers about you is scoped to you and your organization, not shared across other people's conversations. See [Privacy & Data](/help/privacy-and-data).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I trust Magnus's answers without checking?">
    For anything that matters, ask where the answer came from — Magnus can point you to the source document it used. See [Understanding Answers](/help/understanding-answers).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Why did Magnus ask me a question instead of just answering?">
    Either your request was ambiguous and it needed clarification, or it's about to do something that isn't easily undone and wants your confirmation first. See [When Magnus Asks You](/collaboration/when-magnus-asks-you).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can Magnus take actions in tools like Slack or Salesforce, not just answer questions about them?">
    If your organization has connected those tools and granted the specific action, yes — and for many tools, it acts through your own connection, so the action shows up as coming from you. See [Connected Tools](/capabilities/connected-tools).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What if Magnus says it can't help with something?">
    That's usually a deliberate boundary, not a bug you can work around by rephrasing. See [Troubleshooting](/help/troubleshooting).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Where do I go if something seems actually broken?">
    See [Troubleshooting](/help/troubleshooting) for common cases, and your organization's support channel for anything that isn't resolved there.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
