title: "Getting Things Done"
description: "Using Magnus for tasks, not just questions"
---

Magnus isn't limited to answering questions — you can ask it to actually produce something or carry out a multi-step task, and it'll work through it and hand you a result.

## What this can look like

- "Put together a one-page summary of this document."
- "Pull together a list of our open opportunities for this account and format it as a table."
- "Draft a message I can send to the team about this."

For more involved requests, Magnus may take a little longer while it works — reading source material, checking facts, and assembling the result — rather than answering instantly. That's normal for anything more involved than a quick lookup.

## Getting a file back

When a task produces something concrete — a document, a report, a file — Magnus delivers it to you directly in the conversation as an attachment you can open or download. It doesn't just describe what it made; you get the actual file.

<Warning>
  Only trust a file or link that actually appears as an attachment in the conversation. If Magnus's text mentions a link that doesn't show up as a real, clickable attachment, treat that as a sign something went wrong rather than a working link — ask it to try again, or see [Troubleshooting](/help/troubleshooting).
</Warning>

## Setting Magnus up to succeed

- **Break down genuinely large asks.** "Reorganize our entire knowledge base" is a lot to hand over in one message — a narrower, concrete task ("summarize these three documents") tends to go better.
- **Say what "done" looks like.** If you have a format or length in mind, say so up front rather than after the fact.
- **Expect a check-in for anything risky.** See [When Magnus Asks You](/collaboration/when-magnus-asks-you) — Magnus will confirm before doing something that's hard to undo.
