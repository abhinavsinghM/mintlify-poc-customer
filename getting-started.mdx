## Just start typing

There's no special syntax or command to learn. Talk to Magnus the way you'd message a knowledgeable coworker:

- "What's our policy on expense reimbursement for travel?"
- "Who's the account owner for Acme Corp in Salesforce?"
- "How many vacation days do I have left?"
- "Can you put together a short summary of our Q3 roadmap doc?"

Magnus reads your message, figures out what kind of help you need, and either answers directly or quietly brings in the right specialist (HR, knowledge search, Salesforce, and so on) to handle it — you don't need to know or care which one.

## Tips for a good first message

- **Be specific.** "What's the leave policy" gets a more useful answer than "help," and "the leave policy for the UK team" is better still if it applies to you.
- **Give context Magnus can't already know.** If you're asking about a specific record, project, or document, name it.
- **It's fine to ask follow-ups.** Magnus keeps track of what you've already discussed in the conversation — see [Memory](/collaboration/memory) — so you can say "and what about for contractors?" without repeating yourself.
- **You can ask it to do things, not just answer questions.** "Draft a short summary of this thread" or "pull together a list of open opportunities for this account" are reasonable asks — see [Getting Things Done](/collaboration/getting-things-done).

## If Magnus doesn't have what you need

Sometimes the honest answer is "I don't have that" or "I'm not able to help with that" — that's a real answer, not a bug. See [Understanding Answers](/help/understanding-answers) for why, and [Troubleshooting](/help/troubleshooting) if something seems to have gone wrong rather than Magnus just not knowing.
