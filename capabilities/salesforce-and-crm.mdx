title: "Salesforce & CRM"
description: "Look up accounts, contacts, leads, and opportunities without switching tabs"
---

If your organization has connected Magnus to Salesforce, you can ask about CRM data directly in conversation instead of digging through the Salesforce UI:

- "Who's the account owner for Acme Corp?"
- "What's the status of the opportunity with Initech?"
- "Give me a summary of our contacts at Globex."
- "What leads came in this week for the enterprise segment?"

## What Magnus can look up

- **Accounts** — company records, ownership, key details
- **Contacts** — people associated with an account
- **Leads** — inbound prospects and their status
- **Opportunities** — deals in progress, their stage, and who owns them

Magnus reads the live data behind these records rather than a stale summary, so an answer about "the current stage of this deal" reflects what's actually in Salesforce right now.

<Note>
  Magnus only sees the Salesforce data you're already entitled to see — connecting it doesn't widen your access, it just makes the access you have easier to use in conversation.
</Note>

## Getting a useful answer

Name the account, contact, or opportunity as precisely as you can — "the deal with Acme" is easier for Magnus to resolve correctly than "that deal we talked about."
