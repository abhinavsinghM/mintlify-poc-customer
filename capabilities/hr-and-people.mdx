Magnus can help with the everyday questions you'd otherwise send to HR or dig for in a handbook:

- "How much PTO do I have left this year?"
- "What's the process for requesting parental leave?"
- "Who's my manager on record?"
- "What benefits am I enrolled in?"

## Two kinds of HR answers

Some questions Magnus answers from your company's HR **policies and documentation** (see [Finding Information](/capabilities/knowledge-search)) — things that are true for everyone, like "what's the policy."

Others depend on **your own record** in connected HR systems (for example, a system like Workday). For those, Magnus looks up the actual data behind the question rather than reciting a general policy — so "how many vacation days do I have left" gets *your* number, not a generic answer.

<Note>
  Magnus only sees the HR data your organization has connected it to, and only what you're entitled to see. It can't answer questions about someone else's personal HR details.
</Note>

## Getting a useful answer

Be specific when your question depends on details like location or employment type — leave policy, for instance, often varies by country or team. If you don't specify and it matters, Magnus should ask rather than assume.
