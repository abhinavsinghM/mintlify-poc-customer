## How Magnus works with you

Magnus isn't just a search box — it's built to work *with* you over a conversation:

- It **remembers** things you tell it, both within a conversation and, when useful, across future ones. See [Memory](/collaboration/memory).
- It can **do things**, not just answer — like putting together a document or report you can download. See [Getting Things Done](/collaboration/getting-things-done).
- It **asks when it needs to** — a clarifying question, or your confirmation before something that can't be easily undone. See [When Magnus Asks You](/collaboration/when-magnus-asks-you).
- It **tells you where an answer came from** when it searches your company's knowledge, rather than asking you to just trust it. See [Understanding Answers](/help/understanding-answers).
